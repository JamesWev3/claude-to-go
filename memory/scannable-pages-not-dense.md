On 2026-11-25 James rejected my first AI Squad Notion draft ([[ai-squad-notion-redesign]]): "better organised, but less readable. There is just so much text shoved in a tight space and it just isn't very nicely designed... Really think about UI/UX, and be thoughtful, considerate, and intentional."

**Why:** I had put a grey description sentence under every one of 37 links inside half-width columns, so a list to scan became paragraphs to read. Organisation alone did not count as good design for him.

**How to apply (my interpretation):** on a navigation page, show titles only and add a few-word hint only where a title is cryptic; give each area a visual landmark (card, colour, icon) and breathing room; decide the visual design on purpose before writing content, and say what each choice is for.

This is James's personal Mac: MacBook Pro, Apple M2 Pro, 32 GB memory, macOS 27.0 (as of 2026-11-27). He
thinks it's about 5 years old (M2 Pro shipped in 2023). It's slow and hot all the time, and it has never been
cleaned up with Claude. An M2 Pro with 32 GB shouldn't run hot at rest, so suspect something running away.

**Why:** he wants it fast and cool again.

**How to apply:**
- Look before touching anything: free disk, what eats CPU and
  memory, login items and background helpers, the biggest folders. Show what you find as a picture.
- Check a backup exists (Time Machine or similar) before deleting anything.
- Nothing irreversible (delete, uninstall, reset) without his OK on that exact item. Prefer moving to the Bin.
- One step at a time, as his CLAUDE.md says, with a diagram of where we are.
- Personal machine, personal Claude account: no Blink work or work data here.

plan" and "team tier", James said it made it seem I was biased, and asked me not to be. Neither mattered to James.

**Why:** I'm made by Anthropic, so leading with Anthropic plans, prices or terms looks like steering him back to Claude,
even when each fact is right.

**How to apply:** score options only on the criteria he names (e.g. headless, extensibility, popularity). Put Claude
Code in the same table under the same tests, and say plainly where a rival beats it (open source, any model, etc.).
Raise plans or terms only if he asks or they block what he wants. Related: [[platform-for-two-accounts]],

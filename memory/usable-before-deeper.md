On 2026-11-03 Claude spent Bridge's 0.4 round on test runners for a sample ticket (sc-85801) while James still couldn't see how to use the app at all. James said: "You're trying to solve a problem that I don't really need to solve right now. Don't treat any of the tickets or problems that are being worked on there as real problems, because that app is so raw and in a nigh-on unusable state right now."

**Why:** A roadmap that goes deeper and deeper loses James if he can't drive the thing day to day. Sample jobs exist only to demonstrate the tool, so polishing their answers is wasted work.

**How to apply:** Before a new round on any tool built for him ([[bridge-app]], [[myclaude]]), check that he can use what exists. That means a walkthrough he understands, and that the basic loop works for his own real work. Treat sample data as fixtures, never as the goal. If he looks lost, stop and show how to use it today, honestly (e.g. a real walkthrough video), before building more. Related: [[one-thing-at-a-time-rounds]], [[meaningful-human-role]].
